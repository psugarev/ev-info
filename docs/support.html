EV Info — Support

Read-only battery and OBD2 telemetry for Hyundai Ioniq 5 / E-GMP cars. Everything runs on your iPhone; there is no account and no server.

What you need

  • iPhone with iOS 17.6 or later.
  • Bluetooth LE ELM327 adapter — tested with vLinker iCar Pro BLE 4.0. Classic-Bluetooth-only adapters and Wi-Fi adapters are not supported.
  • Car powered on (accessory or ready) so the OBD port is alive.

First connection

  1. Plug the adapter into the car's OBD2 port (under the dashboard, driver's side on the Ioniq 5).
  2. Open EV Info and tap the status header on the Drive tab, or the gear → OBD2 adapter.
  3. Tap Rescan for OBD2 adapter, then pick your adapter from the list.
  4. The header narrates each stage: Initializing ELM327 adapter… → Configuring read-only OBD session… → Selecting vehicle OBD protocol… → Checking vehicle data availability… → Preparing first BMS read… → OBD ready.
  5. No adapter nearby? Switch on Demo mode (gear → Demo mode, or the "Use demo data" button on Drive) to explore every screen with recorded data.

Troubleshooting

“Bluetooth permission denied” — iOS Settings → EV Info → Bluetooth, then reopen the app.

No adapter in the list — move the phone within ~1 m of the adapter, make sure it is not paired with another phone, and rescan. Entries older than five minutes are dropped.

Values grey out — that is the stale badge: the app has not received BMS data for 3 seconds. Check the adapter is still powered and connected.

Speed shows “—” — OBD speed is not exposed by this car's standard PID set; GPS speed is used while recording a trip.

Data and privacy

Everything is stored on the device. See the privacy policy. Settings → Storage lets you limit retention or delete all data.
