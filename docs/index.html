EV Info

Read-only battery and OBD2 telemetry for the Hyundai Ioniq 5 / E-GMP family. State of health, all 192 cell voltages, charging power, tire pressures, trips and real electricity cost — computed on your iPhone. Nothing is uploaded.

What it needs

  • An iPhone with iOS 17.6 or later.
  • A Bluetooth LE ELM327-compatible OBD2 adapter (for example vLinker iCar Pro) plugged into the car's OBD port.
  • No account, no subscription, no cloud.
